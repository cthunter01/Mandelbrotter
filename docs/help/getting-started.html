Getting started

Mandelbrotter opens on the whole Mandelbrot set. Roll the mouse wheel over any part of its edge and the picture zooms in around the pointer: a coarse version appears at once and sharpens over the next moments. Drag with the left button to move around. That is most of what there is to know; the rest of this guide is detail.

Try it: jump into Seahorse Valley, then Try it: back to the whole set

The side panel

Every setting lives in the panel on the right. From top to bottom: the fractal family and Julia mode, the iteration limit, the colouring, the orbit overlay and your bookmarks. Changing a setting re-renders the picture (or just recolours it, when only the colours changed). View > Show side panel (Ctrl+B) hides and shows the panel.

The status bar

From left to right: the complex number under the pointer; the centre of the view; the zoom, with "(deep)" added when deep rendering is in use; the iteration limit in use; and "Rendering..." or "Rendered in N ms" for the picture on screen. Coordinates show at most sixteen decimals followed by "..." when there are more.

Speed

Rendering uses every processor core. A picture appears in four passes (blocks of 8, 4 and 2 pixels, then every pixel); anything you do while it renders cancels the render and starts a new one, so the window never waits for you. If you built Mandelbrotter yourself, a debug build renders several times slower than a release build; see the README for the release preset.
